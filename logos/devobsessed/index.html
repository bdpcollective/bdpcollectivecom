Software Consulting Company Logo

Logo design for a software development consulting company I work for.

Back to Logos

Project Overview

I created a logo for DevObsessed, a technology consulting company focused on modernization, cloud migration, and development acceleration.

Objectives

  • Reflect DevObsessed's modern, professional, and approachable brand
  • Use a clean, strong design that feels technical without being cold
  • Create something flexible for digital and print use

Design Approach

  • Color Palette: Teal, white, and dark teal for a sharp, modern look
  • Typography: Bold, clean lettering for strong readability
  • Iconography: Subtle technical elements to hint at innovation without clutter

Outcome

The final logo gives DevObsessed a distinctive, polished identity that stands out in the crowded tech consulting space. It's simple, memorable, and easy to use across websites, marketing materials, and event branding.

Deliverables

Deliverable Details
Logo Formats SVG, PNG, and EPS
Color Variations Full color, black, and white versions
Usage Examples Website header, business cards, LinkedIn banners

About
